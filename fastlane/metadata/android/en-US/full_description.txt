<i>Unofficial app, not affiliated with or endorsed by Obsidian.</i>

<b>TickOff</b> puts the tasks of your Obsidian vault on your home screen.

<b>Task list widget</b>
• Shows the open tasks ("- [ ] …") of a Markdown file as a checklist
• Pick a different file for each widget when you add it, e.g. one for work, one for shopping
• Tap the box to tick a task off. TickOff writes "- [x]" and today's done date ("✅ yyyy-mm-dd", the Obsidian Tasks style) into the file
• Tasks with a 📅 due date are sorted: overdue 🔥, today 🌟, then upcoming; tasks due beyond a window you choose are hidden
• Optional: tap a task to open the file in the Obsidian app, either with Obsidian's built-in link or with the Advanced URI community plugin
• Follows your phone's light or dark theme

<b>Voice capture widget</b>
• A one-tap mic button: dictate your tasks, and they are added to the same file
• Relative dates like "next Friday" become proper 📅 due dates
• Uses your own OpenAI or Claude API key (bring your own key). Only the transcribed text is sent, never audio

<b>Private by design</b>
• No account, ads, analytics or tracking
• Accesses only the vault folder and the files you pick, through Android's pickers (no storage permissions)
• The task list works fully offline. The network is used only for voice capture, directly between your phone and the AI provider you chose
